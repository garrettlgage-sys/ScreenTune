Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · ScreenTune by GLGCUSTOMS

The short version: ScreenTune doesn't collect any information about you.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • Your settings stay on your own PC
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected

What ScreenTune does

ScreenTune is a Windows app that adjusts your display's colors: vibrance, saturation, gamma, contrast, brightness, color temperature and color balance. It does this through Windows display settings and your graphics card driver.

Information we collect

None. ScreenTune does not collect, record or transmit personal information, usage data, device identifiers, screen contents, files, or data from any other app or game.

Information stored on your device

ScreenTune saves your color settings and presets in a small text file in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\ScreenTune\settings.txt, or the Microsoft Store app's private storage). This file never leaves your PC. Uninstalling ScreenTune removes it, or you can delete it yourself at any time.

Internet connections

  • The Microsoft Store version of ScreenTune makes no internet connections. The Microsoft Store delivers its updates.
  • The direct-download version only connects to the internet if it checks for updates. That check downloads a small version file and sends no personal information. As with any web request, the server hosting that file may see your IP address in its standard logs.

Purchases

If you buy ScreenTune through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the payment under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. GLGCUSTOMS does not receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

ScreenTune collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy
